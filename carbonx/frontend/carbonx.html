End-to-end Methodology

From Green Action to Verified Impact

Every environmental contribution follows a transparent, cryptographically assured journey.

Act

Submit

Upload verifiable telemetry evidence, sensor payloads, and cryptographic metadata of your environmental activity.

Verify

Decentralized MRV verifiers and satellite telemetry engines review data and compute certified CO2 equivalence.

STEP 04

Receive minted on-chain Carbon Credits (CC) directly in your vault to hold, trade, or redeem for real benefits.
